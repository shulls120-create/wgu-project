Brief History of the Golden State

California is a state located on the west coast of the United States. It is known for its diverse geography, which includes beaches, mountains, and deserts. California is also famous for its entertainment industry, particularly Hollywood, as well as its technology sector in Silicon Valley. The state has a rich cultural history and is home to many iconic landmarks and attractions.

California had become the 31st state in 1850, two years after the Mexican War had happened. From there, the state became a gold mine in a literal sense with the start of the Gold Rush.

*Fun Fact*

California is the most populous state in the United States, with over 39 million residents as of 2020.

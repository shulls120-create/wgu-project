San Francisco: The Golden Gate City

Information

  • Population: 826,079 as of 2025
  • Median household income: $140,970 as of 2024
  • Cost of living: 64% higher than the national average
  • Major attractions: Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz Island, Fisherman's Wharf, Lombard Street, Chinatown

*Fun Fact*

The Golden Gate Bridge isn't gold or red; its official color is International Orange, chosen because it stands out in thick fog and blends well with nature.

San Francisco was founded in 1849 during the Gold Rush. It grew rapidly and became a major center for the tech industry in the late 20th century.

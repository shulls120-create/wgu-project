Los Angeles: The City of Angels

Information

  • Population: 3,869,089 as of 2025
  • Median household income: $81,939 as of 2024
  • Cost of living: 43-52% higher than the national average
  • Major attractions: Universal Studios Hollywood, Griffith Observatory, Los Angeles County Museum of Art, Hollywood Sign, The Broad

*Fun Fact*

The very first electronic message was sent over the ARPANET. ARPANET was considered the precursor to the internet we have today.

Los Angeles was founded in 1781 by no more than 44 settlers. It wasn't long until the city grew and grew. It was first under the controls of Spain and Mexico before finally coming under control of the United States. It eventually became one of the largest metropolitan cities in the world, with it's vibrant culture, night life, historical attractions and rich history.

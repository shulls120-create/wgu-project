San Diego: The City of Dreams

Information

  • Population: 3.3 million
  • Median household income: $$108,077
  • Cost of living: 47% higher than the national average
  • Major attractions: Balboa Park, USS Midway Museum, San Diego Zoo, La Jolla Cove, Coronado Beach

*Fun Fact*

San Diego is the city with the largest avacado production than any other county or place in the United States.

San Diego was founded in 1769 by Spanish colonists. It wasn't until 1850 that it became an official city. It grew into a bustling, vibrant city with plenty of attractions such as the San Diego Zoo!
